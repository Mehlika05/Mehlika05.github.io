chocolate cake

a delicious chocolate cake with cherries on top. Use cherries garnish!

ingredients

  • Chocolate Cake Ingredients
    • 2 cup flour
    • 1½ cup cocoa powder
    • 2 eggs
    • ⅓ cup of oil
    • 1½ cup of milk
    • 1 teaspoon baking soda
    • 1 teaspoon baking powder
    • 1½ teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 Fahrenheit.
  2. place a piece of parchment on a 12 inch x 10 inc baking sheet.
  3. Prepare the cake:
    1. whisk the flour, cocoa powder, eggs, baking soda, baking powder, salt in a large bowl.
    2. Pour oil and egg. Stir until combined. Pour into the flour mixture and mix until batter is just combined.
  4. Pour the batter into the baking sheet.
  5. Spread the batter.
  6. Bake the cake for 25 minutes.
  7. Remove the cake from the oven.
  8. You can serve now. Enjoy your meal.
